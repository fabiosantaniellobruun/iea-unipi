## The steps

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| At least 6 months before the graduation session | Send the Teaching Office ([didatticadestec@ing.unipi.it](mailto:didatticadestec@ing.unipi.it)) the [preliminary thesis assignment request form](https://www.destec.unipi.it/sites/default/files/attachments/Modulo%20richiesta%20preliminare%20tesi.pdf) (PDF, in Italian), signed by your supervisors, the Programme Chair and the other required signatories. |
| Within 30 days of the session | Submit your graduation application on the Alice portal, following the [University instructions](https://www.unipi.it/index.php/laurea-ed-esame-di-stato/item/1616-informazioni-sulla-domanda-di-laurea) (external site, in Italian). |
| A few weeks before the session | You receive the instructions for the defence from the Programme Chair by institutional email. |
| On the day of the session | You present your thesis in a public defence of up to 20-25 minutes. |

## When you graduate
Graduation sessions are held in June, July, October, December, February and April. The exact dates are in the [School of Engineering calendar](https://www.ing.unipi.it/it/studenti/calendario-didattico/2026-2027) (in Italian).

## The thesis
The thesis is an original piece of work describing a significant design activity, carried out under the guidance of a supervisor. You can work on it in the Department, at public or private research bodies or in design practices, in Italy or abroad. You can also write it in English.

## The final grade
1. **Starting point:** the credit-weighted average of your marks, expressed out of 110 and rounded to the nearest whole number. Extra exams and activities without a mark out of 30, such as internships and workshops, do not count.
2. **Final examination:** from 0 to 11 points.
3. **Extracurricular activities** relevant to your studies: from 0 to 2 points.

## If you need to postpone
If you have already submitted your application and want to move your graduation to a later session, inform the Student Registry Office **within 15 days of the session date**, as stated on the University website.
