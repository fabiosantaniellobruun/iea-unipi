From 18 to 26 October 2025 the Fortezza da Basso in Florence hosts the 15th Florence Biennale. The Design Days take place on **23 and 24 October**, with architecture and design studios and, on the 23rd, a meeting with Patricia Urquiola, who receives the "Leonardo da Vinci" International Lifetime Achievement Award.

The two mornings are curated by Prof. Gabriele Goretti (DESTEC), director of the Industrial Design programme at the University of Pisa.

Students can enter with a discounted ticket. Information: Prof. Goretti.
