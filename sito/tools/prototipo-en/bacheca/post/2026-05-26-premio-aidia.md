The Italian Association of Women Engineers and Architects (AIDIA) rewards projects and professional experiences with a positive, concrete impact on the environment, society, technology and organisation, in the spirit of sustainability, innovation and equal opportunities. The award is linked to Goal 5 of the UN 2030 Agenda, Gender Equality.

**Who it is for:** women researchers, professionals, PhD candidates, graduates and local organisations.

## Categories
1. Regeneration and redevelopment
2. Environmental improvement
3. Digital systems
4. Women's managerial leadership
5. Fondazione Inarcassa special award for women freelance professionals

The winner of each category receives €1,500.

**Deadline:** 21 September 2026.
