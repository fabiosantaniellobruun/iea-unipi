The Department of Civil and Industrial Engineering is running a special teaching project on the conservation of marble artworks. You will work in groups on cases inspired by real ones from the Opera del Duomo of Pisa, to design and build restoration prototypes using:
- 3D modelling;
- additive manufacturing (3D printing);
- composite materials for marble conservation.
