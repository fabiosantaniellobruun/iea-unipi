title: ET CONTEST, Beyond Underground: ideas competition
slug: et-contest
sommario: International competition for the surface design of the Einstein Telescope at the former Sos Enattos mine, in Sardinia. Prizes of up to €8,000.
---
The competition asks for ideas to transform the former Sos Enattos mining site (Lula, Sardinia), a candidate to host the Einstein Telescope, the future European gravitational-wave detector. Proposals concern the surface design, with a large-scale masterplan and some graphic guidelines, in dialogue with the landscape and history of the area.

It is promoted by the University of Cagliari (DICAAR) with the Region of Sardinia.

**Who it is for:** students of Architecture, Engineering, Design and related subjects; young graduates, researchers and professionals under 41, also in teams.
