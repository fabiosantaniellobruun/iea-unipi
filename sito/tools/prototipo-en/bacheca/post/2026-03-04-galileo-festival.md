The 13th Galileo Festival takes place in Padua from 8 to 10 May 2026, with experts in the space economy, artificial intelligence, robotics, health technologies and innovative companies.

The Festival **Academy** offers 200 grants for university and ITS students. They include reserved access to events, accommodation, meals and transfers, a certificate of attendance and a €60 book voucher.

| Option | Duration | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Guided company visits + Festival | 7-10 May, 3 nights | €360 |
| Festival only | 8-10 May, 2 nights | €260 |

**First application deadline:** 29 March 2026.
