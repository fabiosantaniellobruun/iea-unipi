Architect and researcher **Prachi Mehar** (New Delhi) talks about sustainability that is not only environmental and technical but also human: vernacular intelligence, nature-inspired design, well-being and ecology, materials and circular design.

It is the closing activity of the special teaching project *Social Habitat Architecture: teaching studio for lightweight construction*, funded by the University of Pisa for 2026.
