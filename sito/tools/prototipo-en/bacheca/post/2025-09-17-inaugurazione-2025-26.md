The Programme Chair, Prof. Luca Lanini, opens the academic year 2025/26 with a lecture by architect **Fabrizio Barozzi**, of the Barozzi Veiga studio.

Barozzi presents the reuse of Groeninge Abbey in Kortrijk, Belgium, now *Abby Kortrijk*, an art museum conceived as an open and inclusive urban space.
