## How it works
1. **Read the call for applications.** It is published every year between June and July and sets out places, dates and fees.
2. **Register for the test** on the University of Pisa's Alice portal and pay the participation fee by the deadline.
3. **Take the test.** It is the same throughout Italy for Architecture and Architecture and Building Engineering programmes.
4. **Check the ranking list** and, if you are admitted, complete your enrolment.

To enrol you need an upper secondary school diploma.

## The test
The test consists of 50 multiple-choice questions, to be answered in 100 minutes.

| Area | Questions |
|---|---|
| Reading comprehension | 10 |
| Knowledge from school studies and history, including art history | 10 |
| Logical reasoning | 10 |
| Drawing and representation | 10 |
| Physics and mathematics | 10 |

**Scoring:** +1 for each correct answer, −0.25 for each wrong answer, 0 for each unanswered question. Maximum 50 points.

If you are admitted with a score below the set threshold, you will have additional learning requirements (OFA) to make up in the first year, according to the programme's [Academic regulations](/studiare/regolamenti/).

**To prepare:** on the website of CISIA, the consortium that runs the test, you will find [a description of the ARCHED test](https://www.cisiaonline.it/altri-test/test-arched) and [a practice area](https://allenamento.cisiaonline.it/utenti_esterni/login_sso.php) (in Italian). The Ministry also publishes [a sample test with answers](https://accessoprogrammato.mur.gov.it/compiti/CompitoArchitettura2019.pdf) (PDF, in Italian).

### How to register for the test
On the [Alice portal](http://www.studenti.unipi.it/):
1. register or log in. You will need the tax code on your health card, an identity document and a passport photo;
2. go to *Segreteria* and then *Iscrizione concorsi*;
3. choose Ingegneria Edile-Architettura;
4. pay the fee from the *Tasse* section. [How to pay](https://matricolandosi.unipi.it/tassa-concorsuale/) (in Italian).

At the end you will receive a receipt with your test registration number.

**Remember:** if you do not pay the fee by 23 August 2026 you cannot take the test.
