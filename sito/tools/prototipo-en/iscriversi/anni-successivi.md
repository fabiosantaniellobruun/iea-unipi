Every year the University publishes a call to assign the vacant places from the 2nd to the 5th year.

## Who can apply
- Students enrolled in the same programme at another Italian university who request a transfer.
- Students enrolled in another programme at the University of Pisa who request a change of programme.
- Students enrolled in another programme at another university, in Italy or abroad, who request a transfer.
- Graduates, in Italy or abroad, who request enrolment with credit recognition.
- Students enrolled in this programme under a system prior to Ministerial Decree 270/04 who request to switch to the new system.

The minimum requirements for each year are listed in the table in Article 4 of the call.

## How to apply
1. Read the call carefully.
2. Register on the [Alice portal](http://www.studenti.unipi.it/) and sign up for the competition *SER, Ingegneria Edile-Architettura anni successivi al primo*.
3. Pay the participation fee.
4. Check the ranking list on the University page.
