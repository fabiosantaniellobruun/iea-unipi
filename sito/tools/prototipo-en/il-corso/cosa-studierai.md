## Subjects
| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Basic** | Mathematics, physics, drawing, history of architecture |
| **Core** | Architectural, structural and urban design; restoration. With attention to environmental comfort, energy and environmental impact |
| **Related** | Hydraulics, geotechnics, socio-economic aspects, construction site management |

The year-by-year details are in the [study plan](/studiare/piano-di-studi/).

## Studios, workshops and seminars
Alongside the lectures, the programme organises studios, workshops and seminars. The aim is to get you used to working across disciplines, as happens in professional practice. Design studios account for a large share of the credits every year and attendance is compulsory.

## Experiences abroad
You can spend part of your studies abroad: with the Erasmus programme, with internships in professional practices or by preparing your thesis at another university. Find out how on the [International mobility](/studiare/mobilita/) page.

## Beyond the lectures
Every year the programme offers lectures and meetings with architects, teachers from other universities and professionals. Since 2014 it has also organised the [international Summer School "The city and the water"](/studiare/summer-school/). You will find the schedule on the [noticeboard](/bacheca/eventi/) and other initiatives under [Opportunities](/studiare/opportunita/).
