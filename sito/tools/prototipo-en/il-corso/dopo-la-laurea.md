## Two professional associations, one degree
After the State Examination that qualifies you for the profession, you can register with:
- the Order of Engineers (Ordine degli Ingegneri);
- the Order of Architects, Planners, Landscape Architects and Conservators (Ordine degli Architetti, Pianificatori, Paesaggisti e Conservatori).

Thanks to the European recognition of the degree, you can practise as an architect in all Member States of the European Union.

Information on the State Examination: [School of Engineering page](http://www.ing.unipi.it/it/dopo-la-laurea/esame-di-stato) (in Italian). [DA VERIFICARE: aggiungere il riferimento all'esame di Stato per architetto]

## What you will be able to do
Work as a freelance professional, or in positions of responsibility in public bodies and private companies involved in building and transforming cities and landscapes. The main activities:
- designing buildings, urban spaces and landscapes;
- restoring buildings and monuments;
- planning cities and territories and assessing the environmental impact of plans and projects;
- managing construction works, sites and companies, including their economic side.

## Further study
- **PhD**, in Italy or abroad. [PhD programmes at the University of Pisa](https://www.unipi.it/ateneo/statuto-e-regolamento/regolamenti-delle-strutture/corsi-di-dottorato/)
- **University Master's programmes**: intensive postgraduate courses to specialise or update your skills. [Master's programmes at the University of Pisa](https://www.unipi.it/didattica/corsi/master/)
- **Postgraduate advanced courses** (corsi di perfezionamento).
