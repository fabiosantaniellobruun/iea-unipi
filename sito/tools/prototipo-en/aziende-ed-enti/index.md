## How to host an internship
1. **Sign an agreement with the DESTEC Department.** The Department handles the administrative side of internships for the programme. Follow the procedure on the [University of Pisa internship portal](http://tirocini.adm.unipi.it/) (in Italian), in the section for companies and organisations.
2. **Wait for activation.** The agreement is active once it has been signed by both parties and registered on the portal.
3. **Fill in the training project online.** Log in to the portal with your credentials and enter the objectives, location, duration and insurance cover of the internship.
4. **Follow the internship.** A company tutor supports the intern; an academic tutor follows the educational aspects. The project is signed by both tutors and by the intern.
5. **Write the final report** on the skills acquired.

Paying a monthly allowance is not compulsory.
