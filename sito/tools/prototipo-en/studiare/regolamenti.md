## Studio submissions and mid-term tests
Internal rules approved by the Programme Council on 15 March 2018.

- Submissions and mid-term tests are scheduled **outside the exam sessions**, both ordinary and extraordinary.
- The periods set aside are the two weeks before the end of the 1st semester and the first and last two weeks of the 2nd semester.
- Before each semester, teachers publish a calendar of submissions and tests, divided by year. Dates can only be changed by agreement between the teachers and the students of that year.
- In the design studios, models must be easy to carry. If a scale larger than 1:100 is needed, it can be agreed to build only part of the model.
- The number and format of drawings must match the complexity of the project.
- To print your drawings you can use the DESTEC computer centres (Architecture building and Hydraulics building).
