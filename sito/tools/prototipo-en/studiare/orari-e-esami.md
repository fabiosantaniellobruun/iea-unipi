| Period | Dates |
|---|---|
| 1st semester, 12 weeks | from Monday 21 September to Saturday 12 December 2026 |

### Extraordinary exam rounds
Reserved for students who have exceeded the standard duration of the programme, they are held during teaching periods:
- from Monday 26 October to Saturday 28 November 2026;
- from Monday 22 March to Friday 30 April 2027.
