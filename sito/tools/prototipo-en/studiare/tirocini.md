## What it is
An internship gives you direct experience of the world of work while you study. It is **curricular** because it takes place during the programme and is part of the study plan: in the 5th year it is worth 3 or 6 CFU, among the [elective activities](/studiare/piano-di-studi/#5-anno). An internship carried out to prepare your thesis is also curricular.

## Main rules
- It can last up to 12 months, including any extensions. Specific rules apply to students with disabilities.
- It is based on an **agreement** between the company or body and the DESTEC Department.
- Before you start, the company fills in an online **training project** with objectives, location, duration and insurance cover.
- The project is signed by you, by a company tutor who supports you at work and by an academic tutor, a teacher of your choice who follows the educational aspects.
- At the end, the company writes a report on the skills you have acquired.
- The company is not required to pay you an allowance.

The rules follow the University of Pisa regulations and national legislation (Interministerial Decree no. 142 of 25 March 1998).
