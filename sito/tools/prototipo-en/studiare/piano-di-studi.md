Each year follows the plan of its own cohort, that is, of the year in which students enrolled. For the syllabus, teachers and exam format of each course, see the [course catalogue](https://unipi.coursecatalogue.cineca.it/corsi/2026/10302/insegnamenti?codicione=0500107311900002).

## 1st year
*For students enrolling in 2026/27*

| Course | CFU |
|---|---|
| Mathematical Analysis 1 | 6 |
| Architectural Drawing 1 | 12 |
| Building Materials Technology and Applied Chemistry | 6 |
| General Physics | 6 |
| Geometry | 6 |
| History of Architecture 1 | 9 |
| Theory and Techniques of Architectural Design | 6 |
| English language test, level B2 | 3 |
