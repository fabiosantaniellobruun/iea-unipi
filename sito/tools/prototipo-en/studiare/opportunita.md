## Summer School "The city and the water"
Since 2014 the programme has organised an international design school on the relationship between cities and water, full time and in English.

[Discover the Summer School](/studiare/summer-school/)

## Special teaching projects
Hands-on experiences with limited places, often with other degree programmes, which lead to a certificate of attendance. Example for 2026: [Composite materials and digital technologies for the conservation of marble artworks](/bacheca/post/2026-03-18-progetto-speciale-marmo/).
