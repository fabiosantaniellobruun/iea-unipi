The Summer School "The city and the water" is a full-time design workshop organised by the DESTEC Department and the Architecture and Building Engineering programme. Students and young professionals from all continents take part.

## How it works
- **A real case study.** Each edition works on an area connected with water.
- **International groups.** Participants work in groups of different nationalities, to compare approaches and experiences.
- **Two phases.** First a critical reading of the area, to understand its values and problems. Then an enhancement project, supported by theoretical and methodological lectures.
- **Teachers from Italy and abroad.** Teachers and tutors from the University of Pisa and visiting professors from other schools of architecture, engineering and landscape.
- **Final exhibition.** The projects are presented in a public exhibition.

**Who it is for:** students and PhD candidates in architecture, building engineering, urban planning, design and landscape; young professionals and artists, from Italy and abroad. The language is English.
