## Erasmus and University of Pisa programmes
The programme encourages experiences abroad: study with the Erasmus programme, internships in professional practices and theses abroad.

Calls and information: [Study and internships abroad](https://www.unipi.it/index.php/studio-e-tirocinio) on the University portal.

## Erasmus italiano
With the call for mobility between Italian universities, students enrolled in a Master's or single-cycle Master's degree can spend a semester at another Italian university. Applications are submitted on the [Alice portal](http://www.studenti.unipi.it/).

The latest call concerned the 2nd semester of 2025/26, with a deadline of 6 November 2025. [Erasmus italiano call 2025/26](https://www.unipi.it/campus-e-servizi/opportunita-e-tempo-libero/bando-erasmus-italiano-a-a-2025-2026-semestre-2/) (in Italian)
